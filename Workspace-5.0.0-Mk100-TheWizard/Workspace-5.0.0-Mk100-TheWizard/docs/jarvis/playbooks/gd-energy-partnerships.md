# Playbook: GD Energy Partnerships

Core sources: GPT export threads on **GenerativeDrive + Galp**, **Energy Partnership Proposal: GenerativeDrive**, and related BNEF/partnership conversations.

This playbook guides how Jarvis should support Ariel in framing and executing energy partnerships (Galp, utilities, BNEF, others) around GenerativeDrive.

## Objectives

- Present GD as:
  - A credible, technically solid platform for decentralised energy.
  - A strategic lever for partners (Galp, etc.) to lead in new models.
- Map partner incentives:
  - Energy majors: new revenue, brand, decarbonisation.
  - Utilities: stability, smart-grid modernisation.
  - Investors (e.g., BNEF): scalability, innovation, impact.

## Partnership Narrative (Galp Example)

When preparing a partnership pitch (Galp or similar), Jarvis should help Ariel:

- Anchor on:
  - **Interior of Portugal** as focal region.
  - Local cells (1000 houses) as atomic unit.
  - Hydrogen + batteries + smart grid as integrated stack.
- Frame GD as:
  - A **national-scale innovation project** with:
    - Technical backbone already reasoned and simulated.
    - Clear role for a major like Galp in infrastructure, investment, and brand.
  - A lever for **Portugal to lead** in decentralised, AI-optimised grids.

  - Concrete, testable phases (pilot, scale, national roll-out).
