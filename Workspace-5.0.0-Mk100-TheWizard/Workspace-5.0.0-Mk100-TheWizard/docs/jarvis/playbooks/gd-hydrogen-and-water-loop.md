# Playbook: GD Hydrogen & Water-Loop

Core sources: GPT export threads on **GDH2 ROI and Business Model**, **Solar & Hydrogen Solutions**, **Hydrogen Mirai Filling Procedure**, and water/hyperloop concepts.

This playbook captures how Jarvis should reason about hydrogen and water-loop ideas inside GenerativeDrive.

## Hydrogen as Long-Duration Storage

- Treat hydrogen as:
  - A **battery** for seasonal/long-duration storage.
  - Complementary to local electrochemical batteries (short/medium term).
- Jarvis should:
  - Keep track of practical constraints:
    - Refuelling logistics (e.g., Mirai procedure).
    - Safety and regulatory considerations.
    - Efficiency and round-trip losses.
  - Integrate hydrogen models with:
    - Production (electrolysis linked to solar/wind).
    - Storage and conversion back to electricity or fuel.

## Water-Loop / Hyperloop Concept

- Idea: **hyperloop-like systems powered by magnets / Halbach arrays** to:
  - Move water up at end of loop using energy gained from descent.
  - Combine gravitational potential, regenerative braking, and smart control.
- Jarvis’ job:
  - Treat this as an **R&D concept** inside GD, not a first-phase dependency.
  - Keep the idea:
    - Documented.
    - Linked to concrete physics/engineering constraints.
    - Available for future innovation/specialised agents.

## Integration with GD

- In GD simulations and planning:
  - Include hydrogen as one of the core levers in energy storage scenarios.
  - Keep water-loop ideas in a dedicated R&D space; do not over-index them for early-stage investor decks unless requested.
